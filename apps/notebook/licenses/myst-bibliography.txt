Third-party notices for Notebook local bibliography resolution

This inventory covers the pinned Citation.js core/BibTeX runtime dependency
closure, including Node-only dependencies conservatively. The browser artifact
of fetch-ponyfill also embeds whatwg-fetch 3.5.0. No citation-js-utils,
@citation-js/plugin-csl or citeproc package is included.

The source compiler uses an explicitly forced local BibTeX parser; these
notices do not grant or describe network activity. Existing MyST/parser notices
remain in myst.txt and myst-dependencies-*.txt.
